UwULock Server - third-party notices
====================================

UwULock Server is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only (see LICENSE). Its binary and its container image
also carry three icon databases, taken from their upstream repositories at the commits listed in
crates/uwulock-server/icon-databases/sources.json and turned into packs by
scripts/icons/update.mjs (see docs/icons.md). Their licences and notices follow.

The icon library of the web vault also offers selfh.st Icons (https://selfh.st/icons/, CC BY 4.0,
"Icons by selfh.st"). Those are not part of the binary: the server fetches them when they are
asked for.

The password check also hands out the public lists of breached websites of Have I Been Pwned
(https://haveibeenpwned.com/, CC BY 4.0, "Data from Have I Been Pwned") and XposedOrNot
(https://xposedornot.com/). Those are not part of the binary either: the server fetches them once
a day, and every answer and the vault name the source.

The web vault built into the binary carries four fonts, all under the SIL Open Font License 1.1:
UwU Sans (from UwUMail, based on Atkinson Hyperlegible Next), Manrope, Rubik and DM Sans (section 4).

All logos are trademarks of their owners. Showing one does not mean its owner endorses UwULock.

The admin portal's failed logins show where an address is from DB-IP's free databases "IP to
City Lite" and "IP to ASN Lite" (https://db-ip.com), licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
"IP Geolocation by DB-IP". They are not part of the binary or the image: the server downloads
them once a month while the setting `geoip` is on (see docs/failed-logins.md), unchanged.
